# Python Data Analysis for Renewable Energy Systems

A collection of 10 Python exercises exploring renewable energy systems through numerical analysis, data processing, and visualization.

## Tasks

1. **Solar PV Analysis** — Daily generation patterns and statistical analysis.
2. **Wind Power Analysis** — Wind speed, turbine output, and regression.
3. **Electricity Demand and Solar PV** — Net demand and renewable energy contributions.
4. **Solar and Wind Integration** — Energy balances and battery operation.
5. **Battery Storage** — Grid imports, exports, and electricity pricing.
6. **Wind Farm Performance** — Capacity factors and turbine performance.
7. **Microgrid Management** — Renewable integration and diesel consumption.
8. **EV Charging** — Charging demand and emissions analysis.
9. **Seasonal Solar Analysis** — Monthly generation trends and capacity factors.
10. **Energy Efficiency** — Energy savings and payback calculations.

## Tools

Python · Pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Jupyter Notebook

*Educational exercises using simplified datasets.*
